# U.S. Consumer Brand & Product Knowledge v1

Version: `v1.0.0`

## Scope and use

This is an independently curated, public-information knowledge base for a
Trust & Safety demo. It contains factual brand names, aliases, broad product
categories, and two or three representative product families. It does not
contain employer policy, confidential enforcement lists, logo files, inferred
authorization, or a claim that every mentioned product is currently sold.

The 50-brand set is a representative high-recognition sample for U.S.
e-commerce review, not an authoritative popularity ranking. Selection used
U.S. consumer-recognition and brand-value studies as directional signals, then
favored physical-goods categories useful for demo cases. Product-family names
are stable public examples curated on 2026-09-29, and every entry includes an
official brand URL for later verification and maintenance.

Recommended retrieval unit: one `BRAND-*` section. A retrieval hit supplies
entity and product context only; it must never prove infringement by itself.

## Governance notes

1. Brand presence is an entity signal, not violation proof.
2. Product-family similarity does not establish trademark ownership,
   authenticity, or protected-design status.
3. Compatibility, resale, authorization, ordinary-word, and descriptive-use
   contexts must be evaluated before rejection.
4. Official source URLs support factual maintenance; they must not be treated
   as authorization evidence for a seller.
5. Update or retire entries through a versioned candidate review. Do not edit
   the frozen policy or Golden Set merely because this catalog changes.
